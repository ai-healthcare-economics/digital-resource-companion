# Guided Pathways

Use these pathways when the practical task is clearer than the relevant chapter or resource identifier. Select the smallest sufficient set and add specialist resources only when the decision requires them.

## Define a new proposal

**For:** Executives, clinical leaders, project sponsors, and portfolio committees

**Decision question:** What service problem is being addressed, compared with what, and is the institution ready to proceed?

**Book chapters:** 1–2

**Essential resources:**
- **AIHE-A01 — Decision and Use-Case Specification**
- **AIHE-A02 — Stakeholders, Decision Rights, and Participation**
- **AIHE-A03 — Institutional Readiness Assessment**
- **AIHE-A04 — Data Source, Provenance, and Fitness Record**

**Recommended support:**
- AIHE-CASE01 — Emergency-Department Triage Portfolio Decision
- AIHE-CASE02 — Readmission-Model Readiness and Data-Fitness Assessment
- AIHE-RG07 — Common AI Adoption Failures and Corrective Actions

**Required inputs:** Baseline evidence, proposed population and setting, realistic alternatives, known dependencies, and named decision owners.

**Expected output:** A problem-first proposal, comparator set, readiness position, evidence gaps, and a proceed, remediate, pilot, or stop decision.

**Typical time:** 2–5 hours across one or more multidisciplinary sessions.

## Prepare an economic and affordability case

**For:** Health economists, finance teams, HTA specialists, service managers, and commissioners

**Decision question:** Do expected outcomes justify full lifecycle cost and budget exposure, and which uncertainties could change the decision?

**Book chapters:** 6–8

**Essential resources:**
- **AIHE-B01 — Economic Evaluation Specification**
- **AIHE-B02 — Lifecycle Cost and Pricing Record**
- **AIHE-B03 — Benefit Realization and Capacity Conversion**
- **AIHE-B04 — Cost-Effectiveness, Budget Impact, and HTA Record**
- **AIHE-B05 — Model Selection, Uncertainty, and Reproducibility**

**Recommended support:**
- AIHE-CASE06 — Five-Year Lifecycle Cost, Pricing, ROI, and Benefit Realization
- AIHE-CASE07 — Remote Monitoring: Cost-Effectiveness, Budget Impact, and HTA
- AIHE-CASE08 — Imaging Prioritization Decision Model and Value of Information
- AIHE-RG05 — Scenario Planning and Real-Options Worksheet

**Required inputs:** Comparator, population, resource quantities and prices, outcome evidence, uptake, implementation assumptions, and uncertainty ranges.

**Expected output:** A transparent cost, affordability, value, uncertainty, and evidence-priority record.

**Typical time:** One day to several weeks, depending on modelling complexity and evidence maturity.

## Validate a system locally

**For:** Clinical, data, informatics, quality, patient-safety, and research teams

**Decision question:** Does the exact system and version perform acceptably in the intended local population, workflow, and decision window?

**Book chapters:** 2, 5, 8, and 11

**Essential resources:**
- **AIHE-A04 — Data Source, Provenance, and Fitness Record**
- **AIHE-EX05 — Local Validation Report Template**
- **AIHE-B05 — Model Selection, Uncertainty, and Reproducibility**
- **AIHE-C01 — AI Quality-Control and Accreditation Evidence Plan**

**Recommended support:**
- AIHE-CASE11 — Local Validation and Causal Evaluation of a Readmission Model
- AIHE-RG02 — Reporting and Appraisal Tool Selection Guide
- AIHE-RG06 — Diagnostic and Predictive Evidence Appraisal Checklist

**Required inputs:** Versioned cohort and data period, intended use, thresholds, subgroup definitions, workflow, and response-capacity evidence.

**Expected output:** A local-validation report with limitations, subgroup findings, clinical utility, monitoring conditions, and decision recommendation.

**Typical time:** Several days to several months, depending on consequence and study design.

## Evaluate and contract with a vendor

**For:** Procurement, legal, privacy, cybersecurity, clinical, technical, finance, and governance teams

**Decision question:** Which solution and contract best meet the defined need while preserving evidence access, monitoring, continuity, and exit?

**Book chapters:** 6, 9, 10, and 12

**Essential resources:**
- **AIHE-A01 — Decision and Use-Case Specification**
- **AIHE-B02 — Lifecycle Cost and Pricing Record**
- **AIHE-C03 — Integrated Institutional-Risk Review**
- **AIHE-D01 — Vendor Evidence and Contract Requirements**
- **AIHE-D02 — Lifecycle Gates, Staged Implementation, and Dossier Status**

**Recommended support:**
- AIHE-CASE09 — AI Vendor Procurement and Contract Negotiation
- AIHE-RG04 — Regulatory, Standards, and Policy Update Register

**Required inputs:** Service specification, evidence requirements, technical architecture, pricing scenarios, mandatory safeguards, and exit requirements.

**Expected output:** A due-diligence record, vendor comparison, contractual requirements, approval gates, and negotiation position.

**Typical time:** Several multidisciplinary sessions across the procurement cycle.

## Implement and monitor safely

**For:** Clinical operations, quality, informatics, implementation, patient-safety, and governance teams

**Decision question:** How should the intervention be introduced, monitored, corrected, and scaled without losing control of safety, workload, equity, or value?

**Book chapters:** 4, 5, and 12

**Essential resources:**
- **AIHE-C01 — AI Quality-Control and Accreditation Evidence Plan**
- **AIHE-C02 — Corrective and Preventive Action Record**
- **AIHE-D02 — Lifecycle Gates, Staged Implementation, and Dossier Status**
- **AIHE-D03 — Post-Deployment Monitoring and Algorithmovigilance Cadence**
- **AIHE-D04 — Incident Classification and Reconstruction**

**Recommended support:**
- AIHE-CASE04 — Ambient Documentation Assistant: Productivity and Workforce Redesign
- AIHE-CASE05 — AI Quality-Control, Accreditation, and Corrective Action
- AIHE-CASE12 — Model Update, Incident Response, and Renewal Decision
- AIHE-RG03 — Implementation Framework Selection Guide

**Required inputs:** Approved intended use, implementation plan, baseline measures, thresholds, incident process, accountable owners, and fallback pathway.

**Expected output:** A staged implementation, monitoring, incident, corrective-action, and scale decision record.

**Typical time:** Continuous from preparation through routine use.

## Review an update, incident, or renewal

**For:** Quality, safety, technical, clinical, vendor-management, governance, and executive decision authorities

**Decision question:** Does new evidence, an incident, a material change, or renewal justify continuation, redesign, restriction, replacement, or retirement?

**Book chapters:** 5, 9, 10, and 12

**Essential resources:**
- **AIHE-C02 — Corrective and Preventive Action Record**
- **AIHE-D03 — Post-Deployment Monitoring and Algorithmovigilance Cadence**
- **AIHE-D04 — Incident Classification and Reconstruction**
- **AIHE-D05 — Change-Control and Renewal Record**

**Recommended support:**
- AIHE-CASE12 — Model Update, Incident Response, and Renewal Decision
- AIHE-RG07 — Common AI Adoption Failures and Corrective Actions

**Required inputs:** Current system and data versions, monitoring results, incident evidence, changed assumptions, alternatives, costs, and contract position.

**Expected output:** A reconstructed event or change record and a documented continue, restrict, replace, or retire decision.

**Typical time:** Event-triggered, with periodic formal renewal.

## Assess circularity and asset stewardship

**For:** Procurement, supply-chain, clinical engineering, facilities, sustainability, finance, and governance teams

**Decision question:** How can health-system value be preserved while reducing avoidable demand, extending safe useful life, and managing environmental and material consequences?

**Book chapters:** 13–15

**Essential resources:**
- **AIHE-E01 — Circular-Economy and Sustainable-AI Assessment**
- **AIHE-E02 — Circular Procurement and Supplier Scorecard**
- **AIHE-E03 — Asset-Life Decision Record**
- **AIHE-E04 — Circularity-Adjusted Evaluation Record**
- **AIHE-E05 — Environmental, Circularity, and Disposition Dashboard**

**Recommended support:**
- AIHE-CASE13 — Circular AI Infrastructure Strategy
- AIHE-CASE14 — Imaging Platform: Maintain, Refurbish, Replace, or Share
- AIHE-CASE15 — Edge, Cloud, or Shared Diagnostic AI: Circularity-Adjusted Evaluation

**Required inputs:** Functional requirement, asset condition, supportability, resource use, costs, residual value, supply-chain, and disposition evidence.

**Expected output:** A circularity, procurement, asset-option, sustainable-value, and disposition decision record.

**Typical time:** Several hours for screening; several weeks for full lifecycle evaluation.

# Pathways by Role

## Board, executive, or senior manager

Begin with Define a new proposal, then use the economic case, vendor, implementation, and renewal pathways as commitment increases.

## Clinical, nursing, quality, or patient-safety leader

Use local validation, implementation and monitoring, and incident or renewal pathways. Add human-oversight and public-communication records where relevant.

## Health economist, HTA specialist, finance team, payer, or commissioner

Use the economic and affordability pathway, followed by modelling, procurement, and circularity-adjusted evaluation where material.

## Digital health, informatics, data, or technical team

Begin with readiness and data, then local validation, process flows, and the Python Analytics browser.

## Ethics, legal, privacy, cybersecurity, or institutional assurance

Use institutional-risk, vendor, implementation, monitoring, incident, and renewal pathways.

## Procurement, supply-chain, clinical engineering, facilities, or sustainability

Use vendor contracting and circularity and asset-stewardship pathways.

## Educator, facilitator, student, or professional learner

Begin with a worked case and the Common Case-Analysis Method; use reference guides before moving to applied institutional records.
