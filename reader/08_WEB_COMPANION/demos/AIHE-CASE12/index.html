Model Update, Incident Response, and Renewal Decision

Scenario

A vendor deploys version 2.4.0 of a deterioration model. Alert volume rises above review capacity, false positives increase, and three near misses occur because important alerts are delayed. The institution must reconstruct the incident, assess materiality, preserve continuity, and make a renewal decision.

Learning objectives

  • Classify a material model or workflow change.
  • Reconstruct data, version, output, human action, and context.
  • Apply predefined restriction and rollback thresholds.
  • Connect incident learning to renewal and portfolio governance.

Worked interpretation

Version 2.4.0 breaches false-positive, alert-volume, response-time, and near-miss thresholds. Use should be restricted immediately, evidence preserved, and rollback to 2.3.1 performed where safe. Restart should depend on local testing of the model, interface, threshold, and workflow under realistic capacity. Renewal should require improved update notice, audit access, rollback support, and effectiveness evidence.
