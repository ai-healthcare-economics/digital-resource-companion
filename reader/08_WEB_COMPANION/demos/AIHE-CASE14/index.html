Imaging Platform: Maintain, Refurbish, Replace, or Share

Interpretation aid

Scenario

A fictional hospital must refresh an aging imaging-AI platform and associated servers. The options are to maintain the current system, refurbish selected components, replace the platform, or purchase a shared managed service. Clinical suitability, software support, validation, resilience, residual value, and disposition are included.

Learning objectives

  • Compare options using the same functional requirement and horizon.
  • Include software support, cybersecurity, and validation.
  • Recognize residual value and disposition obligations.
  • Use safety and continuity as non-compensatory constraints.

Worked interpretation

Maintaining appears cheapest but is conditional on safety, cybersecurity support, and parts availability. Refurbishment provides a defensible middle option when validation confirms performance and the supplier commits to support. The recommended decision is conditional refurbishment with a funded fallback, take-back agreement, and a replacement trigger if reliability, support, or validation thresholds are breached.
