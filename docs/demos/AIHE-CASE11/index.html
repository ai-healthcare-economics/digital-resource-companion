Interpretation aid

Scenario

A fictional hospital validates a vendor readmission model and implements it with a new care-management pathway. Local discrimination and calibration are weaker than the vendor report, one language subgroup performs poorly, and a control hospital is used to distinguish programme effects from secular change.

What this example demonstrates

  • Assess local validity, calibration, and subgroup performance.
  • Separate model performance from intervention effectiveness.
  • Use a controlled before-after comparison.
  • State limitations and continuing evidence conditions.

Worked interpretation

Local performance does not reproduce the vendor result, calibration is inadequate, and the minority-language subgroup is below the acceptability threshold. The intervention site improves by 2.2 percentage points and the control site by 0.7 points, yielding a difference-in-differences estimate of -1.5 percentage points. This applies to the complete pathway, not the model alone. Recalibration, subgroup safeguards, and prospective evaluation are required before scale.
