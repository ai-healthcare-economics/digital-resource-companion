Interpretation aid

Scenario

A fictional public payer and hospital evaluate AI-supported remote monitoring for high-risk chronic-disease patients. The intervention produces a small modeled QALY gain but requires a substantial implementation budget and sustained clinical response capacity.

Learning objectives

  • Calculate incremental cost, effect, ICER, and net monetary benefit.
  • Model staged uptake and annual budget impact.
  • Separate HTA evidence from reimbursement and local adoption.
  • Identify distributional and capacity constraints.

Worked interpretation

The incremental cost is 250 per patient and the modeled gain is 0.03 QALYs, producing an ICER of about 8,333 per QALY and positive net monetary benefit at a threshold of 50,000. The budget-impact result is different: implementation and uptake create a positive cash requirement. The recommended decision is conditional adoption with staged uptake, funded response capacity, reimbursement clarification, and monitoring of digital exclusion.
