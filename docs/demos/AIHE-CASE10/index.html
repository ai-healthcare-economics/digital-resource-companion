Interpretation aid

Scenario

A fictional hospital proposes a chatbot for appointment guidance, preparation instructions, and basic symptom navigation. It is not intended to diagnose, but users may treat its responses as clinical advice. The governance group must evaluate privacy, prompt injection, unsupported content, digital exclusion, escalation, and accountability.

Learning objectives

  • Integrate ethics, equity, privacy, cybersecurity, and communication risk.
  • Distinguish inherent from residual risk.
  • Define bounded intended and prohibited uses.
  • Require human alternatives and incident communication.

Worked interpretation

The review does not support unrestricted symptom navigation. Several mandatory concerns remain high, especially unsupported advice, escalation failure, and digital exclusion. The chatbot may be piloted for bounded administrative functions using approved content, disclosure, rapid human escalation, accessible alternatives, minimum data collection, and continuous monitoring.
