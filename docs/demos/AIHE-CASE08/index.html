Interpretation aid

Scenario

A fictional radiology service considers an AI worklist-prioritization system for 10,000 studies per year. Benefits depend on prevalence, sensitivity, specificity, earlier treatment, and false-positive follow-up cost. The committee must identify the parameters that drive net benefit and decide whether further evidence is worthwhile.

What this example demonstrates

  • Specify a transparent decision model.
  • Connect diagnostic performance with pathway consequences.
  • Conduct threshold and scenario analysis.
  • Use value-of-information logic to prioritize evidence.

Worked interpretation

The model is favorable only when timely-treatment benefits and avoided downstream costs are realized. False-positive follow-up and the per-study fee are important cost drivers. A silent-mode study is justified when its cost is lower than the expected value of reducing decision uncertainty and when the study measures time to report, time to treatment, false-positive work-up, and capacity effects.
