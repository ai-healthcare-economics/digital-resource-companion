Scenario

A fictional hospital monitors an AI-supported deterioration pathway over twelve months. After a software update in month eight, the false-negative rate and alert volume rise while downtime and complaints also increase. The quality committee must determine whether the pattern is random variation, a material change, or a workflow problem.

Learning objectives

  • Distinguish a monitoring signal from a causal explanation.
  • Use control limits and balancing measures.
  • Integrate technical, clinical, workflow, and patient-reported evidence.
  • Document containment, root-cause analysis, and effectiveness checks.

Worked interpretation

The month-eight change breaches the false-negative limit and coincides with higher alert volume, downtime, and complaints. Immediate containment should restrict the updated version while the team reconstructs data, model, interface, and workflow changes. Closure requires evidence that corrective action restored performance, affected records and users were addressed, and version and update controls were improved.
