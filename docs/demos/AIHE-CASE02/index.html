Scenario

A fictional integrated health system proposes to validate a 30-day readmission model across two hospitals using EHR, pharmacy, laboratory, claims, and community-care data. A readiness review identifies uneven interoperability, delayed claims, incomplete provenance, and limited governance capacity. The team must distinguish remediable gaps from critical conditions that should stop the pilot.

What this example demonstrates

  • Assess readiness using evidence rather than aspiration.
  • Link data fitness and interoperability to economic and safety consequences.
  • Identify non-compensatory readiness failures.
  • Define a capability-improvement plan before pilot approval.

Worked interpretation

The average score suggests partial capability, but the average is not the decision rule. Interoperability and procurement readiness are critical and both fall below the minimum acceptable level. Live use should be deferred while common identifiers, versioned mappings, vendor audit and update clauses, and funded case-management capacity are established. Retrospective profiling may continue because it reduces uncertainty without creating clinical dependence.
