\chapter*{Responsible Use}
These resources support structured analysis, documentation, education, and
institutional deliberation. They do not constitute clinical, legal, regulatory,
procurement, accreditation, cybersecurity, or health technology assessment advice.
Local verification, lawful governance, professional judgement, and accountable
approval remain necessary.
