## Recommended supporting resources

- **AIHE-A — AI Readiness Assessment for Health Systems** — Assess strategic, data, infrastructure, workforce, governance, economic, ethical, security, and monitoring readiness at organizational and use-case levels.
- **AIHE-EX03 — Data Source Map and Access Record** — Document data sources, access authority, interoperability, quality, provenance, and reuse constraints.
- **AIHE-L — Health-System AI Readiness Maturity Assessment** — Rate health-system maturity by domain and identify the weakest critical capability and prerequisites before pilot or scale.
- **AIHE-U — SHARE-AI Secondary-Use Governance Worksheet** — Authorize secondary data use through purpose, harmonization, access, risk, public value, and audit controls.
- **AIHE-Y — Data Quality and Fitness-for-Purpose Assessment** — Assess completeness, plausibility, consistency, timeliness, representativeness, lineage, and actionability.
