\section{Supplemental Resources}
\label{sec:comp-ch09-supplemental}

These supplemental resources translate the Chapter~9 concepts into editable
institutional records. They should be adapted to the intended use and consequence,
completed by a multidisciplinary team, and maintained under version control. They
are not validated clinical, legal, regulatory, procurement, accreditation, or health
technology assessment instruments.

\subsection{Pre-Procurement Requirements}
\label{sec:comp-ch09-supp-1}
Use this record to establish the decision before analysis begins. Complete every
field or explain why it is not applicable. Do not replace missing evidence with an
implicit assumption.

\subsection{Vendor Scorecard and Mandatory Safeguards}
\label{sec:comp-ch09-supp-2}
The following sequence converts findings into accountable action. The local record
should name the responsible person for each activity, the completion date, the
evidence of completion, and any residual uncertainty.

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Define the service problem, comparator set, intended and excluded uses, and measurable outcomes..
 \item Require version-matched evidence and complete technical, data, security, economic, and workflow due diligence..
 \item Apply mandatory safeguards before weighted comparison..
 \item Model pricing and lifecycle cost under realistic volume, update, support, and exit scenarios..
 \item Convert monitoring, audit, update, continuity, portability, and exit requirements into enforceable clauses..
 \item For the existing platform, compare prospective continuation, renegotiation, restriction, replacement, and decommissioning without allowing sunk cost to determine the decision..
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Contract, Renegotiation, and Exit Record}
\label{sec:comp-ch09-supp-3}
A final record should distinguish observations, modelled estimates, value
judgements, and contractual claims. It should preserve the evidence cut-off, exact
system or instrument version, dissent, unresolved risks, monitoring plan, and the
reason the selected action is preferred to the alternatives.
