\section{Supplemental Resources}
\label{sec:comp-ch05-supplemental}

These supplemental resources translate the Chapter~5 concepts into editable
institutional records. They should be adapted to the intended use and consequence,
completed by a multidisciplinary team, and maintained under version control. They
are not validated clinical, legal, regulatory, procurement, accreditation, or health
technology assessment instruments.

\subsection{Quality Monitoring Plan}
\label{sec:comp-ch05-supp-1}
Use this record to establish the decision before analysis begins. Complete every
field or explain why it is not applicable. Do not replace missing evidence with an
implicit assumption.

\subsection{Incident and CAPA Record}
\label{sec:comp-ch05-supp-2}
The following sequence converts findings into accountable action. The local record
should name the responsible person for each activity, the completion date, the
evidence of completion, and any residual uncertainty.

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Reconstruct the change timeline, data sources, model, software, interface, threshold, and workflow versions..
 \item Distinguish statistical stability from clinical and managerial acceptability..
 \item Investigate source-data, interface, threshold, user, staffing, and downstream-capacity explanations before selecting corrective action..
 \item Use the existing quality and patient-safety system to document containment, root causes, corrective and preventive action, and effectiveness checks..
 \item Assess whether the change is material and which validation evidence must be repeated..
 \item Communicate the decision, residual uncertainty, and conditions for return to unrestricted use..
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Accreditation and Organizational-Learning Record}
\label{sec:comp-ch05-supp-3}
A final record should distinguish observations, modelled estimates, value
judgements, and contractual claims. It should preserve the evidence cut-off, exact
system or instrument version, dissent, unresolved risks, monitoring plan, and the
reason the selected action is preferred to the alternatives.
