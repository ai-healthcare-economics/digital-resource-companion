\section{Supplemental Resources}
\label{sec:comp-ch01-supplemental}

These supplemental resources translate the Chapter~1 concepts into editable
institutional records. They should be adapted to the intended use and consequence,
completed by a multidisciplinary team, and maintained under version control. They
are not validated clinical, legal, regulatory, procurement, accreditation, or health
technology assessment instruments.

\subsection{Intervention Specification}
\label{sec:comp-ch01-supp-1}
Use this record to establish the decision before analysis begins. Complete every
field or explain why it is not applicable. Do not replace missing evidence with an
implicit assumption.

\subsection{Comparator and Opportunity-Cost Record}
\label{sec:comp-ch01-supp-2}
The following sequence converts findings into accountable action. The local record
should name the responsible person for each activity, the completion date, the
evidence of completion, and any residual uncertainty.

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Map the present pathway and identify the decision window in which prioritisation could still change care..
 \item Compare all realistic alternatives before assigning value to the AI option..
 \item Represent the intervention bundle: data availability, interface, triage-nurse review, escalation, rapid-assessment capacity, monitoring, and fallback..
 \item Estimate false-positive workload and false-negative consequences at the proposed threshold..
 \item Test whether downstream capacity can absorb the additional patients identified as high risk..
 \item Define a reversible pilot with clinical, operational, economic, equity, and trust indicators..
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Decision Conditions and Review Record}
\label{sec:comp-ch01-supp-3}
A final record should distinguish observations, modelled estimates, value
judgements, and contractual claims. It should preserve the evidence cut-off, exact
system or instrument version, dissent, unresolved risks, monitoring plan, and the
reason the selected action is preferred to the alternatives.
